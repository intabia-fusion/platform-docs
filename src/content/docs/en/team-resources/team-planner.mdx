## Navigating the team planner

The team planner in Intabia Platform provides a way to visualize your team's agenda at a glance. To navigate to it, open the Planner app and pick `Team` from the mode dropdown in the header (`Schedule` / `Calendar` / `Team`).

There's no separate project list in the sidebar: to narrow the view down to one or more projects, use the `Filter` button in the header. This also ensures project privacy: private projects are only visible to their members, while public projects, and all projects for the workspace owner, are visible without restriction.

### Agenda & calendar views

By default, the team planner shows the agenda view for a 3-day window: yesterday, today and tomorrow. You can scroll through the days using the arrow buttons.

The calendar view (`Calendar` in the same mode dropdown) has four modes: Day, Week, Month and Year. Day is only available when the filter narrows the view down to exactly one project; it displays tasks as blocks, in 1-hour, 30-minute or 15-minute increments. Week and Month show each team member's tasks broken down by day; Year shows workload aggregated by month.

## Visibility settings for action items

By default, when a team member schedules an action item in the their personal planner (see [Scheduling action items](/en/task-tracking/scheduling-action-items)), it will automatically become visible on the team planner for all other team members. The title of the action item, associated issue, and number of hours scheduled are all visible. Clicking the dropdown arrow by that task will also reveal the specific time block the action item is scheduled for.

If the team member has set the visibility of their action item to "Only visible to you", it will not appear in the team planner.

Action icon items that are not associated with a space (for example, those that are created manually in an individual's personal planner) will not appear in the team planner. Once an action item has been added to their personal planner, the user can then select a visibility setting for that action item, depending on whether they want it to be visible to everyone, appear as "FreeBusy", or be only visible to them.

## Visibility settings for events

Events on a team member's personal planner will also appear in the team planner, depending on the visibility settings.

If the visibility is set to "Visible to everyone", everyone on the team will be able to see the title, time and duration of the event. If the visibility is set to "FreeBusy", team members will be able to see that person is busy during that time, but they won't be able to see the name of the event.

Just like action items, if the visibility is set to "Only visible to you", the event will not appear in the team planner.

## Visualizing workload and capacity

Intabia Platform does not currently offer a Gantt Chart feature or other visualization of workload and capacity. The team planner does provide a way to see how many hours each team member has scheduled for the day, which can be used in combination with time tracking and due dates to understand project allocations and capacity planning.

If you're interested in finding out more about how you can work with Intabia Platform's current implementation to manage your specific workflows, reach out to your workspace admin to ask questions and exchange ideas.
