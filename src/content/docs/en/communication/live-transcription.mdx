Live transcription is the starting point for an exciting future for Julia AI, who will be able to perform many tasks throughout your workspace to help you work more efficiently.

## Live transcription

When a meeting begins, you'll notice that meeting participants appear in the right side panel while the live transcription appears on the main screen in the `Activity` section. You'll be able to see your transcription appear in real time during the meeting. The transcription language is set in the room's settings.

If you navigate away during the meeting to another part of your workspace, you can still access your live transcription by clicking on the video icon in the right side menu and selecting the `Transcription` tab.

## Disabling live transcription

Live transcription can either be disabled by default for the entire meeting room, or can be disabled for a specific meeting during the call.

To disable the default live transcription for a meeting room, open the room and navigate to `Settings`. Here, you can toggle the default transcription setting on or off.

When transcription is enabled during a meeting, you'll see the quill icon in the bottom menu of your meeting appears red. To disable transcription for a meeting, click the quill icon at the bottom menu (labeled `Stop transcription`). 

> **Note**: Recording is off by default. You can turn it on by default for the entire meeting room the same way as transcription — in the room settings — or toggle it for a specific meeting during the call. To toggle recording during a meeting, click the recording icon in the bottom menu (labeled `Stop recording` while recording is on). The icon will appear red if the meeting is being recorded.

## Viewing past transcriptions

You can see any previously saved transcriptions by navigating to your virtual office and selecting the `Meeting minutes` icon in the top left corner. Here, you'll see a list of all of the transcriptions from your previous meetings. To remove a meeting's minutes from your own list, right-click it and select `Leave` — this leaves the meeting's space rather than deleting the transcription for everyone.

You can also view the transcriptions for a specific room. Click on any meeting room in the office and find the transcription in the `Meeting minutes` secton.

## Privacy

By default, live transcriptions are visible to everyone in the workspace. To restrict access, make the meeting room or the meeting itself private (see "Room security settings" in the [virtual office](/en/communication/virtual-office) guide) — then transcription, recording and chat for that meeting are only available to its members. You can also simply disable transcription (and recording) at the beginning of your meeting, or for the entire meeting room.

## Meeting summary

Besides transcribing, Julia AI can summarize a meeting's main points. Right-click the meeting and select `Summarize discussion`. A comment from Julia AI with the summary appears in the `Activity` section.

## What's next for Julia AI?

In the near future, Julia AI will be able to create activities and documents based on your meetings, help schedule follow-ups and more.

Ultimately, Julia AI will be aware of all workspace activity that she's given permission to see, and thus she will be able to assist in any number of ways that eliminate redundancies, reduce human error and automate tedious tasks to help you work more productively.
