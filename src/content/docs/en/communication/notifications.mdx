Open your notification settings by clicking your profile icon in the bottom left corner and selecting `Settings`, then select `Notifications` from the settings menu.

## Appearance and desktop

The notification settings menu has four sections with global settings: `General`, `Webpushes`, `Appearance` (the unread badge on the chat icon) and `Desktop`. In the `Desktop` section, the main `Show notifications` toggle enables desktop app notifications; while it's off, the notification sound is unavailable. The dock icon bounce and the unread badge count toggle independently.

The `General` section has three delivery channels:
* **Inbox** — a card in the "Inbox" section (always on, no toggle);
* **Push** — a desktop notification;
* **Sound** — a sound alert for events. Sound depends on push: turning sound on also turns push on, and turning push off also turns sound off.

## Settings by group

For finer control, pick a group from the list in the left sidebar: `Notifications`, `Persons`, `Chat`, `Issues`, `Documents`, `Action Items`, `Office` — the list of groups depends on what's enabled in your workspace. Inside a group, each event type has its own toggles for inbox, push and sound delivery — turning off inbox also turns off push and sound for that type.

For example, the `Chat` group has four types: `Join channel`, `Channel messages`, `Direct message` and `Thread message`.
