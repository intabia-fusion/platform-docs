In Intabia Platform Chat, your team talks through channels and direct messages, and also sees activity on documents, issues and other workspace objects right alongside regular conversations. For sending messages through channels and DMs, see [sending messages](/en/communication/sending-messages).

## Viewing workspace activity in chat

The left sidebar of Chat has sections for "Documents", "Issues" and sections for other object types in your workspace. They show objects you've created, are assigned to or a collaborator on, or have been mentioned in.

Comments you add in the "Activity" section of an issue, document or other object show up in its chat in the left sidebar too — and the other way around.

### Viewing an object

When you select a workspace object (an issue, a document, etc.) in the Chat sidebar, it doesn't open as a chat thread — it opens straight into a full view, with all its fields (for an issue, with an attributes panel on the right) and its own activity.

## Mentioning objects in chat

When you mention an object with `@` in a channel or direct message, that message automatically appears in the "Activity" section of the mentioned object. A mention from a direct message or a private channel is visible only to its members; a mention from a public channel is visible to every workspace member. For example, if you discuss an issue in a direct message with a colleague, the messages with the `@` mention appear in that issue's activity, but other workspace members won't see them.
