Intabia Platform builds chat straight into your workspace, so you don't need a separate app to talk to your team. Chat lets you exchange messages in real time through channels and direct messages.

This guide covers how to exchange messages with your team in Chat. Chat also surfaces activity from the rest of your workspace — documents, issues and other objects — alongside your regular conversations. For more, see [activity tracking in chat](/en/communication/chat-activity).

## Ways to chat

There are a few different ways to chat with team members in Intabia Platform:
* **Channels** — public or private chat rooms based on a topic or area of work;
* **Direct messages** — one-on-one or small-group conversations;
* **Other workspace objects** — from Chat, you can access all activity on documents, issues and files. Read more in [activity tracking in chat](/en/communication/chat-activity).

**Icons and indicators:**
* a number next to a channel or chat shows how many messages are unread: red if they triggered a notification, gray if the channel is set to mentions-only or muted;
* a green dot next to a member's avatar means they're currently online.

## Managing channels

To create a channel, click the pencil icon at the top of the Chat sidebar and select `New channel`. Enter a channel name — the `Create` button stays disabled without it — optionally add a topic, pick a visibility — `Public` or `Private` — then click `Create`.

> **Note:** Private channels are visible only to their members — they don't appear in the channels list for anyone else, and there's no way to request access. To add someone to a private channel, a member or owner has to add them directly.

After you create a channel, its settings panel (the settings icon in the channel header) opens on the right with these fields:

<Shot src={channelSettings} alt="Channel settings panel: topic, auto join, name, private, creator and the member list" />

* **Topic** — the channel's topic or description;
* **Auto join** — toggle this to automatically add every new workspace member to the channel. It won't add existing members; only people who join the workspace _after_ this setting is enabled will be added;
* **Name** — the channel's name;
* **Private** — private channels are visible to members only;
* **Created by** — who created the channel (the channel owner by default);
* **Add members** and the member list.

To leave a channel, hover over its name in the sidebar, open the `...` menu and click `Leave channel`, or open "Channels & DMs", right-click the channel and select `Leave`.

A channel can be archived by its owner (the creator, by default), by the workspace owner, or by a member whose role grants the right to archive spaces. Open "Channels & DMs", right-click the channel in the list and select `Archive channel` — the channel's own settings menu doesn't have this option.

> **Note:** Two channels are created by the system when the workspace is created — `#general` and `#random`. They don't have a separate owner, so only the workspace owner or a member with that role right can archive them. For more on permissions, see [roles](/en/advanced-settings/roles).

To star a channel, open its `...` menu and select `Star`. Starred channels appear in the "Starred" section at the top of the sidebar. A direct or group chat can be starred the same way.

`Edit notifications` in the same menu opens the channel or chat's notification mode: `All notifications`, `Just mentions` or `Mute`. In both muted modes, messages still pile up as unread (a gray count next to the chat in the sidebar) but without a notification or sound; `Just mentions` makes an exception for messages that `@` mention you — those still notify you.

## Direct and group messages

To start a direct message, click the pencil icon and select `New direct chat`. Pick one or more people and click `Next`, then click `Create` on the next step. Picking more than one person creates a group chat.

<Shot src={newDmDialog} alt="«Select users» dialog with a list of coworkers and a «Next» button" />

To hide a direct or group chat from the sidebar, hover over it, open the `...` menu and select `Hide from chat list`. Hiding doesn't remove the message history — the chat reappears in the sidebar as soon as it gets a new message. The platform never auto-hides direct messages, group chats, channels or starred chats on its own — it only hides document, issue and other object chats from Chat once they've gone 14 days without a new message and have no unread notifications.

## Message options

Like everywhere else in your workspace, messages support markdown formatting, attachments, `@` mentions, emoji and text templates.

Hover over any incoming message to:
* add a reaction;
* reply in a thread;
* reply in the chat;
* save the message;
* translate it into your interface language (available if the workspace has an AI bot configured for translation). To restore the original text, hover again and click `Show original`.

The `⋮` icon on a message opens more actions: `Forward message`, `Copy link`, `Pin` (a pinned message is shown at the top of the chat), and for your own messages, also `Edit` and `Delete`. A member with the "Maintainer" role or higher can delete anyone's message.

> **Note:** All saved messages are available in the `Saved` tab in the Chat sidebar.

## Chatting in the sidebar

Any channel, direct message or thread can be moved to the right sidebar by clicking `Open in sidebar` in the top right corner of the chat. This keeps several conversations within reach while you work elsewhere in the platform. The chat panel can be hidden by clicking the chat icon in the far right menu.

Your calendar and virtual office are also available through the right sidebar.

## Quick navigation in Chat

At the top of the Chat sidebar you'll find:
* **Threads** — a list of threads you've taken part in;
* **Saved** — all your saved messages;
* **Browser** — search across messages (`Messages`/`Files` tabs, filters by author, place and date);
* **Channels & DMs** — every channel and direct message, with its topic, visibility and member count.
