## List and kanban views

The default view for issues is the list view, where issues are listed in order of their status. Intabia Platform also offers the option for a kanban view, which displays issues in a column format. To toggle between the two views, click either the list or kanban icon in the top left corner of the window, above the issue list.

## Issue peek

Press the `space` bar while hovering over any issue to open that issue's details in the right sidebar. From this view, you can use the up and down arrows on your keyboard to scroll through your issues. Pressing space again closes the panel.

Hovering over comments or due dates will also open a preview of these details.

## Keyboard shortcut

Press `C` on your keyboard to create a new issue from any view.

## Sorting, filtering and searching

Options for sorting and filtering issues can also be found in the top left corner. The `Group & sort` button allows for configuration of grouping and ordering of issues on the page, as well as the `Sub-issues`, `Show empty groups`, `Hide archived` and `Use colors` toggles. The `Customize view` button displays the list of columns shown in the view (priority, component, milestone and others).

<Shot src={toolbar} alt='Issue list toolbar' />

The `Filter` button, next to the search bar, offers options for filtering issues. The search bar can be used to search for a specific issue by keyword.

For quick sorting, select `Active`, `Backlog` or `All` -- these tabs are in the same row as the search bar and filter, on the right edge.

<Shot src={groupSort} alt='Group & sort menu with display toggles' />

## Swim lane

A swim lane is a visual tool for horizontal grouping of issues on the kanban board: each swim lane is a horizontal band for a value of the chosen field (assignee, priority, and so on), which clearly shows how issues are distributed by that field.

In the `Group & sort` -> `Swim lane` menu, you can choose to group by:

* Assignee;
* Priority;
* Component;
* Milestone;
* Status;
* Parent;
* Project.

The list leaves out the field the board's columns are already grouped by: for example, with columns grouped by status (the default), there's no `Status` option.
